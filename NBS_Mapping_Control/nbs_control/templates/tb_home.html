Whole-TB migration reconciliation

Three independent streams are reconstructed from source data and authorised mappings and combined only at final aggregation: A PRODUCT_S3 asset-type balances (CONSOL_KEY + atomic balance type → GL rule), B INTERNAL_CUS_ACC one-to-one accounts (no CONSOL_KEY), C PL with its own key layout. Legacy TB lines without an account-level population use the labelled DIRECT stream (single-target GL mapping only). Missing inputs give SOURCE_PENDING / INCOMPLETE, never a reconciled status. Nothing is plugged or forced to zero.

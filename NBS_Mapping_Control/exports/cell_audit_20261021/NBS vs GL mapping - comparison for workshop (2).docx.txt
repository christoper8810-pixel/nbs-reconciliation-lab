Summary
GL Report and GL Mapping agree in most rows. Exceptions: RECDEFERREDPFT (GL Mapping also lists accrued income), the accrued profit list, late payment fee names, acceptances, PAYDEPOSITPFT and PDOACCOUNT.
The COA sheet is the outlier for ACCDEFERREDPFT and RECDEFERREDPFT and appears to predate the 15 Sep change. NBS follows the COA view in both.
NBS deviates from the configured GL lines in 14 of the 20 rows; acceptances cannot be judged until the GL sheets agree. Decision for the workshop: confirm the GL Report as the reference, correct the GL sheets where they disagree with it (COA rows 1084, 1110, 1112; acceptances; 6513 CREDIT), then align NBS.

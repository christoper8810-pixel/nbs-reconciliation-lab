[29] Issue

[35] LCR_00 C50, C51, C53, C54 (term deposits, "ADD" clause), C86, C87 (loan inflows); NSFR_00 C13, C14

[42] Use the installments scheduled within 30 days (payment schedule); DKB Finance to confirm

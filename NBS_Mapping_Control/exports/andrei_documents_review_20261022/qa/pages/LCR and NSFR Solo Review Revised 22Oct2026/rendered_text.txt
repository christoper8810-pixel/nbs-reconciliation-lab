LCR & NSFR (Solo) – Mapping Review
DKB CBS – QCB liquidity reporting. Prepared for DKB Finance, 21 October 2026. Revised 22 October 2026.
1. Scope and evidence
• Mapping reviewed: LCR & NSFR (Solo) Mapping.xlsx: 22 sheets; the rules sit mainly on LCR_00 and NSFR_00, with supporting tables LCR_01–17 and NSFR_01–04.
• Balances: CAL extract (CONSOLIDATE.ASST.LIAB) at 31 Aug 2026, UAT, used to test whether the rules reach the balances that exist.
• Reference: DKB customer sector codes: Retail 1000–1999, 4000–4004, 6000/6001, 7000/7001; Corporate 2000–2003, 2006, 4005, 5001, 5002; Government 2005; Banks & FI 2004.
Caveat. Findings are based on the rules as written. Amounts are indicative UAT figures showing the size of balances a rule does or does not reach; they are not reconciled report values.
Confirmed mapping changes on 22 October 2026
PRINCIPALPFT: Report related balances with the associated loans, in the same product and customer classification. Cover ACCPRINCIPALPFT, RECPRINCIPALPFT, 
GRCPRINCIPALPFT, DUEPRINCIPALPFT, NABPRINCIPALPFT, DELPRINCIPALPFT, PREPRINCIPALPFT and other locally defined states. Preserve SP status and its loan 
treatment; do not rename SP as ordinary profit. Remove separate accrued-income/other-asset contributions for the same amounts.
Category 3713: Offset the loan balance with the linked category 6050 CREDIT balance. Where ACCCRPROFIT and ACCDEPOSITPFT are mapped together, offset the 
matched pair. Combine the verified signed components once at the linked loan/account level; do not net unrelated accounts or subtract an already negative credit 
twice.
Category 6050: Exclude it from all deposit reporting, including totals, breakdowns, deposit ratios, LCR deposit outflows and NSFR deposit funding. Trace linked 
offsets to the related loan. Flag missing or ambiguous links for investigation; do not put them back into deposits or force a balancing amount.
All changes proposed in NBS01 must be correctly reflected in all three interconnected mappings: NBS01, NBS001 Additional Tables, and LCR & NSFR (Solo). Apply 
the same underlying balance components, linked offsets and category exclusions wherever that population is used, including referenced cells, direct CAL/ECB rules, 
supporting schedules and totals. Preserve each report's required dimensions, cash-flow timing and weights, and reconcile the differences.
The figures below are retained historical UAT observations. They are not recalculated impacts of these changes. Apply the confirmed treatment above wherever an 
earlier recommendation would conflict with it.

4. What is consistent
• Cash uses categories 10000–10025 and 12900–12919, consistent with the other reports.
• Term deposits (6600-6999) are covered by the second ADD clause. Apply CATEGORY <> 6050 to all deposit clauses and their combined totals; retain the maturity correction in A1.
• LCR_00 C9 and NSFR_00 C22 already use the correct maturity direction and can serve as the template for A1.

• Treasury positions are read from the TMS outbound tables (CRSSUKKUK, CRSTBILLST, CRSMMDEALST), as in NBS01/NBS001.

NBS Mapping Review – Issues and Required Changes
DKB CBS – QCB regulatory reporting (NBS01 / NBS001). Revised 22 October 2026.
1. Scope and evidence
Mapping reviewed: NBS MAPPING - 6 Oct 2026.xlsx (copy last saved 7 Oct 2026). A second version (last modified 28 Sep) is in circulation and differs at least in Tables 12 and 40.
Balances: CAL extract (CONSOLIDATE.ASST.LIAB) at 31 Aug 2026, UAT. Amounts in QAR equivalent; two records with impossible amounts excluded.
Reports: NBS01 runs dated 31 Aug 2026 and 7 Oct 2026.
Ledger: Historical trial balance at 23 Sep 2026 (balances apart from one broken record).
Caveat. Amounts are indicative (UAT data, dates differ between sources). Cell references refer to the 6 Oct workbook and must be checked against the configured version before changes are made. Monetary impacts below remain reported UAT observations pending independent same-date reconciliation. Confirm the deployed NBS01/NBS001 rules and the signed gross/net presentation before activation. Owner-defined balance groups do not alone determine regulatory presentation.
Confirmed mapping changes on 22 October 2026
PRINCIPALPFT: Report related balances with the associated loans, in the same product and customer classification. Cover ACCPRINCIPALPFT, RECPRINCIPALPFT, GRCPRINCIPALPFT, DUEPRINCIPALPFT, NABPRINCIPALPFT, DELPRINCIPALPFT, PREPRINCIPALPFT and other locally defined states. Preserve SP status and its loan treatment; do not rename SP as ordinary profit. Remove separate accrued-income/other-asset contributions for the same amounts.
Category 3713: Offset the loan balance with the linked category 6050 CREDIT balance. Where ACCCRPROFIT and ACCDEPOSITPFT are mapped together, offset the matched pair. Combine the verified signed components once at the linked loan/account level; do not net unrelated accounts or subtract an already negative credit twice.
Category 6050: Exclude it from all deposit reporting, including totals, breakdowns, deposit ratios, LCR deposit outflows and NSFR deposit funding. Trace linked offsets to the related loan. Flag missing or ambiguous links for investigation; do not put them back into deposits or force a balancing amount.
All changes proposed in NBS01 must be correctly reflected in all three interconnected mappings: NBS01, NBS001 Additional Tables, and LCR & NSFR (Solo). Apply the same underlying balance components, linked offsets and category exclusions wherever that population is used, including referenced cells, direct CAL/ECB rules, supporting schedules and totals. Preserve each report's required dimensions, cash-flow timing and weights, and reconcile the differences.
The figures below are retained historical UAT observations. They are not recalculated impacts of these changes. Apply the confirmed treatment above wherever an earlier recommendation would conflict with it.
